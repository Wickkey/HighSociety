Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

High Society is a free, open-source hobby project: an online version of the High Society auction card game. It has no ads and never sells or shares your data for marketing. This page explains what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your username. Every player picks one, as a guest or with Google.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google tells us your Google account ID, email address and name. We store the ID and email to recognize you next time. We use your name only to suggest a username. Your email is never shown to anyone.
  • Your games: who played, the final scores and placements, every bid and pass, your Elo rating over time, achievements, and when you joined and last played. This is what powers your profile, the leaderboard and game history.
  • A sign-in token for each device you use, so nobody else can play under your name. We store only a one-way fingerprint of it, never the token itself.

Chat messages and emoji reactions are not stored. They're passed live to the other people at your table and then gone.

What's public

Your username, Elo rating, stats and game history (including the usernames of who you played) can be seen by anyone who visits your profile. Google-linked accounts appear on the leaderboard. Your email address is never public.

What's saved in your browser

Your username, your Google account ID (if you signed in with Google) and your sign-in token are saved in your browser's local storage and a cookie, so you stay signed in. The site also saves recent stats there so screens load faster, and a short-lived code that lets you rejoin a game after a refresh. Clearing this site's data signs you out.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used: things like the pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location. It uses cookies to do this. See Google's privacy policy. Most ad and tracker blockers turn it off, and the game works the same without it.

Who handles the data

  • Render hosts the website (servers in the United States).
  • Supabase hosts the database (servers in the United States).
  • Google provides sign-in and analytics.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to the site, as part of Render's hosting.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your account and game records are kept for as long as the site runs, so ratings and history stay accurate. To have your account deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page with your username. Please don't post your email or anything else personal there; the issue is public. Deleting an account removes your username and, for Google accounts, your Google ID and email.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions are welcome on GitHub.
