How is the winner decided?

First, anyone with the least money left is eliminated from winning entirely. If two or more players are tied for the least money, none of them are eliminated. Everyone stays in contention. Among whoever remains, the highest total points wins. That total is calculated in two steps, in order: first add up the value of every painting you hold plus a Passe's −5 (if you hold one); only then does any Prestige Card (×2) or Scandale (½×) you hold multiply that whole total. Ties for the highest score are possible and simply win together.
