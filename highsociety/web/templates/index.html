How is the winner decided?

First, anyone with the least money left is eliminated from winning — unless there's a tie for least, or only one player is left standing. Among whoever remains, the highest total points wins (a painting's value, plus a Passe's −5, all multiplied together by any Prestige/Scandale cards held). Ties are possible and are left as ties.
