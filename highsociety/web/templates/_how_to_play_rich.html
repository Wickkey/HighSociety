Goal Auctions Cards Try it

Goal: End the game with the most points — but there's a catch. When the game ends, whoever has the least money left is eliminated from winning entirely. If two or more players are tied for the least money, none of them are eliminated — everyone stays in contention. Only then, among whoever remains, does the highest score win.

Spending every last chip can lose you the game outright, no matter how many points you're sitting on.

Two kinds of auction

Normal auction

Paintings, the Prestige Card

Players take turns raising the bid. Passing means you're out. The last one standing wins the card and pays their bid.

Disgrace auction

Faux Pas, Passe, Scandale

Same bidding, opposite result: the first player to pass gets stuck with the card and keeps their money. Everyone else forfeits what they raised.

In a disgrace auction, passing early is winning. Raising the bid is the risky move — it's the one you don't want to win.

Money committed to a bid is locked in until the auction resolves — you can raise it later, but can't take it back. Anything you don't win with is returned to you.
